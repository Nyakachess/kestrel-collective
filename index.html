The Challenge

A degree should lead to more than a qualification.

Universities face increasing pressure to demonstrate their programmes prepare students for employment. Meanwhile, graduates enter a competitive market where organisations frequently demand practical experience alongside academic qualifications.

Apprenticeships and other routes are giving students alternative ways to gain qualifications and workplace experience simultaneously — universities need stronger ways to connect academic learning with real work.

Kestrel connects these needs.

Kestrel creates structured project opportunities that allow graduates to develop through practical work while helping universities and organisations deliver useful digital and technology outcomes.

  • Accessible entry — no CV or portfolio required
  • Clearly scoped partner projects
  • Practical preparation before delivery
  • Guided matching and support throughout
  • Progress monitoring and communication
  • Measurable outputs and outcomes

What could a Kestrel project look like?

Student-Engagement Dashboard

How It Works

A structured process. At every step.

01

Share Your Challenge

Tell Kestrel about the project, problem or area in which your organisation needs support. No need to have every detail worked out before making contact.

02

Scope the Project

Kestrel works with you to define realistic outputs, timescales, support requirements and measures of success.

03

Prepare & Match Graduates

Kestrel introduces the project to suitable participants and prepares them for the work. Matching considers interests, developing skills, availability and support required — not polished CVs.

04

Deliver With Support

Graduates complete the project while Kestrel provides coordination, guidance and communication with your organisation throughout.

05

Review the Outcome

Kestrel and the partner review what was delivered, what graduates learned and what should happen next.

For Graduates

Your first opportunity should not require years of experience.

Kestrel is for final-year students and recent graduates who want to apply their knowledge, explore digital work and develop through real projects.

You don't need:

  • A professional CV
  • An existing portfolio
  • Previous employment in technology
  • Personal industry connections
  • Years of experience

You should be:

  • Willing to learn
  • Reliable and communicative
  • Open to feedback
  • Committed to completing work
  • Interested in developing practically

Projects may initially be part-time, short-term or fixed-duration. Where projects are paid, this will be stated clearly.

About Kestrel

Built from an understanding of education and technology.

Kestrel Collective was founded by Beryl Odingo, an MSc Computing graduate with a background spanning both education studies and technology. Beryl has worked directly with students, understands the pressures on universities to demonstrate graduate outcomes, and has experienced first-hand the gap between academic learning and professional opportunity.

Kestrel was built to bridge that gap — not by pointing graduates towards job boards, but by giving them something real to work on, with the structure and support to succeed.
